About

I am a researcher working on embodied intelligence, with a focus on world models and robot learning. I’m interested in how agents can learn the dynamics of the physical world and use that understanding to act.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Durham University, advised by Prof. Yang Long. My earlier work on depth estimation, dynamic 3D reconstruction, and generative modeling forms the foundation for my current research.
